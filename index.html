Ever heard of rock, paper, scissors? Here is an an explanation about it.
Rock, paper, scissors is a simple game usually played between two people.
It's often used as a quick decision making method and also for fun. Each
player simply forms one of the three shapes with their hand:

  • Rock
  • Paper
  • Scissors

The outcome is determined by the following rule:

  • Rock crushes scissors
  • Scissors cut paper
  • Paper covers rock
  • If both choose the same object, then it's a tie
